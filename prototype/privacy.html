Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026. One Link is currently in closed beta testing.

This Privacy Policy explains how One Link ("One Link", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the One Link Studio website and app at one-link-studio.netlify.app (the "Service").

1. Data we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Content you create in One Link, such as videos, images, captions, and drafts.
  • Connected social-media account data. When you connect an account through a platform's official login, we receive only the information you authorize, plus access tokens that let us publish content you choose. This may include:
    • TikTok (Login Kit): open ID, display name, and avatar (user.info.basic), and permission to upload videos (video.upload).
    • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): your name, the Pages and Instagram business accounts you choose, and permission to publish to them.
    • WhatsApp (Meta WhatsApp Business): your business phone number ID and permission to send messages you create.
    • LinkedIn: your name, profile photo, email, and permission to share posts.
    • X (Twitter): your username, profile info, and permission to post on your behalf.
  • Basic technical data, such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

2. How we use data

We use your data only to provide the Service: to sign you in, show which accounts are connected, and publish or upload content when you request it. We never post to your accounts without your action.

3. Data we share

When you publish, your content is sent to the platforms you selected (for example TikTok) under your own account and subject to their privacy policies. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share data from any connected platform (TikTok, Meta, LinkedIn, or X) with third parties for advertising.

4. Data retention and control

We keep your data only while your account is active. Disconnecting a social account deletes its stored tokens. You may also revoke One Link's access at any time from each platform's settings (for example TikTok "Manage app permissions", Facebook "Apps and Websites", LinkedIn "Permitted services", or X "Connected apps"). You can request export or deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing us; we complete deletion requests within 30 days.

5. How to delete your data

To delete all data One Link holds about you, including data received from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, LinkedIn, or X:

  • Disconnect the account in One Link under Profile → Connected accounts (this deletes its tokens immediately), and/or
  • Email makarymartin9@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from your account email.

We confirm by email and complete deletion within 30 days.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and access tokens are encrypted at rest. No internet service is perfectly secure, and beta features may change.

7. Children

One Link is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: makarymartin9@gmail.com
