Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. One Link is currently in closed beta testing.

1. Data we store

Account details you provide (name, email), content you create, and access tokens for social-media accounts you connect. Tokens are stored encrypted and used only to perform actions you request, such as publishing your content.

2. Data we share

When you publish, your content is sent to the platforms you selected (for example TikTok) under your own account and their privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

3. Data control

Disconnecting a social account removes its stored tokens. You may request export or deletion of your data at any time through the contact below.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and sensitive tokens are encrypted at rest. No internet service is perfectly secure, and beta features may change.

5. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact us at the email listed on the main page.
