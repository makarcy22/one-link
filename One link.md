\# ONE LINK

\#\# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

\*\*Product:\*\* One Link  
\*\*Positioning:\*\* Create once. Optimize once. Publish everywhere.  
\*\*Primary Users:\*\* Content creators and businesses  
\*\*Product Type:\*\* All-in-one social media creation, publishing, trend discovery and analytics platform

\---

\# 1\. Product Vision

One Link is an all-in-one platform that allows creators and businesses to \*\*create, improve, discover, publish and monitor social-media content from one place\*\*.

Instead of moving between different social-media applications, editing tools, analytics tools and trend platforms, users can use One Link as their central social-media workspace.

The core experience is:

\> \*\*CREATE → OPTIMIZE → DISCOVER → PUBLISH → MONITOR → IMPROVE\*\*

The goal is to make social-media content creation and distribution significantly simpler.

\---

\# 2\. Core Promise

\#\#\# “Create once. Reach everywhere.”

A user should be able to:

1\. Create content inside One Link.  
2\. Upload existing content from their device.  
3\. Improve the content using One Link's assistant.  
4\. Discover relevant trends and hashtags.  
5\. Generate captions, hashtags, mentions and keywords.  
6\. Adapt the content for different platforms.  
7\. Publish to connected social platforms.  
8\. Monitor performance from one dashboard.  
9\. Receive recommendations for their next content.  
10\. Repeat the process.

\# 37\. Product Philosophy

One Link should not simply be:

\> “Another social-media scheduler.”

It should become:

\> \*\*The place where a creator or business goes before, during and after publishing content.\*\*

Before publishing:

\*\*What should I create?\*\*

During creation:

\*\*Help me make it.\*\*

Before publishing:

\*\*How can I improve it?\*\*

Publishing:

\*\*Put it everywhere.\*\*

After publishing:

\*\*How did it perform?\*\*

Then:

\*\*What should I create next?\*\*

That complete loop is the heart of \*\*One Link\*\*.

\# 39\. Success Criteria

One Link should ultimately make users feel that they can manage their social-media presence without constantly switching between multiple applications.

The strongest sign of success will be when a user can go from:

\> \*\*“I don't know what to post.”\*\*

to:

\> \*\*“One Link gave me an idea → I created it → optimized it → published it everywhere → and now I understand how it performed.”\*\*

That is the central product experience.
